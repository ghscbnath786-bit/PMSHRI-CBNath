Institutional Ethos

Empowering Every Learner with Practical Knowledge and Safe Values

"At PM SHRI Govt. High School CB Nath, our vision extends far beyond textbook memorization. With an active enrollment of 190 bright students, our focus is providing equitable, high-standard education grounded in the National Education Policy (NEP 2020). We combine experiential science education, digital smart classrooms, and vocational training in electronics with moral grounding."

"Our school stands strictly as a Tobacco-Free Educational Institution (ToFEI) and conducts regular anti-drug awareness drives under Nasha Mukt Abhiyan. With our thriving herbal garden, compost pits, and green school initiatives, our children learn responsibility towards both society and the environment."

Indigenous Herbal Garden

Biodiversity & Botanical Learning

Zero-Waste Compost Pits

Organic Waste Recycling

All organic school waste and fallen leaves are systematically composted on-site. Students observe the decomposition cycle, generating rich organic manure for campus greenery.

🧪

Soil Testing Facility

Hands-On Agriscience

Equipped with testing equipment where students analyze pH levels, soil moisture, and nutrient compositions, linking classroom chemistry to local agricultural practices.
